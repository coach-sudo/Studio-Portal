# Wave 4 Finance reporting contract

| Measure           | Authoritative records and inclusion rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gross collections | One posted, non-account-credit `payment_entries` payment per ledger row. Provider idempotency remains governed by the unique external reference and existing settlement commands. Includes verified package purchases with a linked package.                                        |
| Refunds           | Posted non-account-credit refund entries. A refund remains a separate event on its recorded local date, even when the payment was in an earlier period.                                                                                                                             |
| Net collections   | Gross less monetary refunds by currency. A later refund can make a reporting period negative.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Outstanding       | Issued open and partially paid invoices less posted payments, entitlements and waivers. Includes independently verified uninvoiced lesson balances through the existing payment context. A reserved invoice checkout remains unsettled and is shown once, with its checkout status. |
| Overdue           | The unpaid portion of an issued invoice once its explicit due date is before the current studio date. An uninvoiced lesson has no inferred due date.                                                                                                                                |
| Scheduled         | A future planned attempt under an active, authorized mandate, with current payer and studio authorization, unchanged lesson terms and an amount within the applicable cap. Scheduled amounts may overlap outstanding balances and have no effect on cash totals.                    |
| Package purchases | Posted monetary payments linked to the purchased package. An assigned entitlement or later redemption creates no second purchase.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Credit activity   | Signed quantities by ledger kind and effective package. The owning student and covered student remain separately attributed when they differ. The existing adjustment kind includes corrections and restorations.                                                                   |

Cash dates use `payment_entries.created_at`. Reporting periods include both calendar dates in the studio timezone and translate to a half-open timestamp interval, including daylight-saving shifts. Receivables and scheduled collections are current snapshots. Their date filters apply to explicit due dates or scheduled charge dates. Currencies are never converted or summed together. CSV amounts are integer minor units or credit quantities, with currency and reporting context on each row.

Service attribution follows verified lesson receipts, direct-checkout lesson identities, a uniquely linked manual booking lesson or the lines recorded in an invoice settlement. Posted allocation amounts may split an invoice payment between services when the amounts reconcile to the ledger row. A service filter selects only that service’s allocated amount. Unfiltered history lists the payment once. A line spanning multiple services without explicit sub-allocations remains Unattributed. Open lessons with ambiguous price, settlement or allocation are omitted from the verified balance.

Reports use one authorized dataset for aggregation, pagination and CSV. Exports cap at 5,000 matches and ask the coach to narrow filters above that cap. The exported fields exclude payment credentials, internal references and recipient addresses.

Attention uses stable studio, issue kind and underlying record identities. A coach read or interaction refreshes current authoritative evidence for that studio. Acknowledge persists while the same evidence remains valid. Snooze ends on expiry or material change. Only informational expiry notices are dismissible, with a reason. A cleared source resolves its issue, and recurrence reopens the same identity. The interaction history is internal. Existing invoice delivery, checkout, payment and credit commands retain their own authorization and execution.
