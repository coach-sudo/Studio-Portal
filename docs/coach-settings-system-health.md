## Evidence and operating limits

| Area            | Authoritative evidence                                                                                                                                                    | Interpretation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Calendar | Studio-owned projection rows joined through the lesson, open synchronization conflicts, and an authenticated CalendarList read for the deployment-owned selected calendar | Fresh projections within 72 hours establish completed synchronization. A CalendarList read verifies accessibility and distinguishes read-only access, authorization failure and throttling. A verified calendar list role alone does not verify permission for every event or prove an event was written. |
| Gmail           | Studio-owned outbox status, eligibility suppression, and durable delivery receipts                                                                                        | Recent accepted mail within 72 hours establishes provider submission. Recipient delivery and provider-side bounces are unavailable. Email where submission started stays held if the response or local receipt is lost.                                                                                   |
| Stripe          | Signed webhook receipts attributable to a studio invoice, booking or training payment attempt, and failed training autopay attempts on studio-owned lessons               | A verified recent linked successful payment event within seven days establishes observed processing. Missing, unattributed or provider-side events require the Stripe dashboard and operator logs.                                                                                                        |
| Jobs            | Booking maintenance receipts and existing worker flags                                                                                                                    | A completed run within 20 minutes establishes freshness. The view includes the most recent attempted and successful execution where recorded. Calendar and email processing use their existing queue evidence. Provider intake is shown as intentionally disabled where configured.                       |
| Access          | Current studio coach membership                                                                                                                                           | The read verifies the selected studio has coach access. Student and household permissions remain in the authorized People workflows.                                                                                                                                                                      |

The Netlify monitor is scheduled every 15 minutes. It reads stored evidence and leases up to three eligible studios per invocation, oldest automatic attempt first. Production defaults to enabled only on the designated site. Protected acceptance can opt in explicitly with `STUDIO_HEALTH_MONITOR_ENABLED`. An unpublished candidate may lack scheduler invocations. Monitor freshness is visible after 35 minutes, independent of manual checks. Provider polling is limited to manual Calendar checks. A provider rate limit disables repeat live probing for five minutes. A bounded source failure leaves other results available and retains the affected service’s unresolved issues. Observations store allowlisted fields, open incidents deduplicate by service and source identity, and resolved incidents retain 90 days of history. At most 40 source problems per service are captured on each run. Saturated results do not mark omitted issues resolved.

Check now is coach-only and scoped to the selected studio. A shared 45-second database lease fences scheduled and manual checks. A 30-second cooldown coalesces repeated manual requests. Each database evidence call has a bounded request, and Google token renewal and CalendarList access share a 3.5-second deadline. The latest successful probe is fresh for up to 24 hours. Diagnostics do not dispatch a worker, send an email, create an event, charge a card, modify bookings or write student data. The active check writes only health snapshots, incident state and monitoring timestamps.

## Repair-action matrix

| Service         | Direct repair and verification                                                                                                                                                                                 | Guided external action                                                                                                                                                               | Read-only coverage and technical escalation                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Calendar | Eligible transient failure queues one existing lesson projection. The RPC preserves event identity and rejects held conflicts and active claims. Worker completion is observed separately.                     | Check sharing and selected calendar in Google Calendar. The operator renews the deployment Google authorization or updates the selected destination. Run Check now after the change. | No in-app OAuth callback or destination selector exists. Series and missing-event conflicts require review, and intake stays disabled.                                                   |
| Gmail           | Only an eligible transactional message with a proven pre-submission failure can be queued. The existing worker rechecks recipient and consent rules. A durable provider boundary fences uncertain submissions. | Review the record and current authorized recipient. Use provider Sent history for uncertain acceptance.                                                                              | No provider delivery or bounce feed, no blanket retry, and no resend of a possibly submitted message. Historical failures lacking proof are operator review.                             |
| Stripe          | No payment mutation, webhook replay, charge retry or settlement repair is exposed.                                                                                                                             | Open the affected invoice, booking or lesson and compare it with Stripe. The operator checks account configuration and webhook delivery.                                             | Linked processing failures and recent success, plus observed failed scheduled payments. Global unattributed events, account flags and absent Stripe deliveries require technical review. |
| Background jobs | Existing scheduler owns retry and lease recovery.                                                                                                                                                              | Share the failed worker receipt and timestamps with the operator.                                                                                                                    | Booking maintenance execution, stale or failed receipts, worker configuration and monitored queue backlog. No generic rerun, no enable toggle and no arbitrary command execution.        |
| Access          | Established student and household workflows handle available permission edits.                                                                                                                                 | Coach sign-in recovery and Google account security use their existing entry points.                                                                                                  | No role-administration panel, identity-provider repairs or session controls were available to reuse.                                                                                     |

Recovery revalidates studio ownership and coach membership, locks the issue and source, and audits the queue result atomically. Cooldowns cap one retry per issue and ten per studio per 15 minutes. Pending and running states retain the issue. The display distinguishes confirmed recovery from disappearance from the observation window. The Dashboard selects at most three fresh serious warnings and one per service and links to the same issue. Finance Attention retains its separate state.

Copy diagnostics includes the stable issue ID, allowlisted source and recovery references, error category, evidence times, release identity and concrete next step. It excludes tokens, raw provider payloads, email content, recipient addresses and student financial details.

## Verification

Focused tests cover connection state, freshness, unavailable evidence, bounded run behavior, authorization rejection, issue identity, safe retries, the Gmail submission fence, and sanitized diagnostics. The PostgreSQL suite passed 32 assertions on an isolated Wave 4 migration baseline. The upgrade expectations preserve historical outbox records with an unset submission fence. See [coach_system_health.sql](../supabase/tests/coach_system_health.sql).

Local Chromium acceptance used real synthetic Supabase Auth accounts and the application’s actual diagnostics and recovery handlers. A coach could administer the selected studio, and adult students, dependents, authorized payers, schedule-only contacts, unrelated users and coaches from another studio received 403 on its diagnostics and repair routes. The separate coach could read their own studio. Check now completed in 150 ms in the local fixture with simulated Google. Fingerprints of bookings, payments, credits, projections, outbox and delivery receipts stayed unchanged across the automatic and manual checks. The run also verified keyboard use, unsaved changes, copy, exact Dashboard deep links, queued recovery and confirmation from new evidence. Axe found zero violations in the three captures and none overflowed horizontally.
