## Existing server contracts and their limits

- Use `student_credit_coverage` for an authorized student, including pagination, its verified individual package projections, and lesson-level `coverageStatus`. Use `household_credit_access` with the authorized portal projection for shared lots and deduplicate by package ID. The future backend must use the same internal ledger projections with an explicit authorized studio and a unique pool identity. The aggregate student summary cannot be summed into a shared balance.
- Low-credit eligibility must use each compatible lot's **available** amount. A reservation reduces availability and can still cover its original lesson. A resolved condition is one whose live eligible state has ended after issuance, reservation, cancellation, policy changes, or passage of the lesson time.
- A planned shortage requires a future scheduled lesson with an explicit credit policy and a current `needs_credit` state. `covered` requires a valid hold. `awaiting_reservation` means a candidate credit exists. `processing` identifies an unsettled purchase or assignment. `studio_review` and `expiration_review` require staff review. Confirmed separate-payment or pay-as-you-go arrangements are outside credit shortage conditions. For untyped manual lessons, the portal's current fallback state cannot prove a separate payment arrangement or a credit obligation.
- Use the existing Stage B reconciliation evidence and open case states for disputed accounting. Unknown purchased terms or unresolved allocation must suppress demand. A coach-facing case list can identify review work, but it carries no authorization for automated delivery.
- Resolve a lesson recipient through the verified billing contact for that lesson's student and applicable communication permission. A shared pool condition still needs an approved pool billing recipient rule. Student copies need separate role-and-preference filtering. Never derive household ownership from a shared email or guardian relationship.
