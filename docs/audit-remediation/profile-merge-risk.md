Do not fix this by deleting recovery jobs, receipts or credit accounts, dropping constraints, or retrying a production merge. Inventory every foreign key and historical receipt identity, plus payer/guardian account relationships and external subscription/customer ownership. Reproduce baseline behavior with jobs, accounts, invoice balances, subscriptions, messages/resources and replay receipts. Verify all-or-nothing failure and preservation before proposing a fix.
