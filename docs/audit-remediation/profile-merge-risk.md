These are schema-confirmed risks requiring isolated fixtures; no historical cascade loss is claimed as reproduced. The [metadata evidence](evidence/student-merge-fk-readonly.json) contains table/constraint definitions only, with no student rows.

Do not fix this by deleting recovery jobs, receipts or credit accounts, dropping constraints, or retrying a production merge. Inventory every foreign key and historical receipt identity, plus payer/guardian account relationships and external subscription/customer ownership. Reproduce baseline behavior with jobs, accounts, invoice balances, subscriptions, messages/resources and replay receipts. Verify all-or-nothing failure and preservation before proposing a fix.

The user approved the identity-preserving design in [decisions](decisions.md) for draft implementation on October 28: an identity-preserving merge/alias can retain historical student IDs and associate current navigation with a keeper; a physical merge would require explicit rules for financial account ownership, immutable receipt replay, incompatible external customer identities and every dependency. A safe interim command may reject unsupported merges before mutation with actionable review guidance. No financial consolidation policy or destructive merge implementation is approved here. The approved design retains all original identities and per-record access; its implementation and acceptance remain pending.
