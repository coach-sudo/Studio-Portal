### Root causes and unified model

Use one read projection per explicitly authorized student, plus an aggregate payer view assembled from the same projections. Preserve the distinction between a lesson occurrence, a booking, an issued invoice line, a settlement, a coverage fact, a recurring booking series, a package subscription and an automatic-payment mandate. Keep the participant link as the student-to-booking join for shared occurrences. Resolve recurring membership only through matching studio, student and recorded identifiers. Record the series boundary separately from the last actual confirmed occurrence and keep exceptions in occurrence history. Conflicting records enter a review state.

For payment, use stored invoice line allocations and booking/payment state as evidence. Authoritative coverage can suppress payment; unassigned package eligibility and unclassified rates require review. Expose Paid or Unpaid as the student-level language, with separate contextual status for processing, coverage, reversals or review. Keep raw credit movements in coach tools. Allow accessible transaction descriptions and billable dates for authorized payers while protecting private lesson content and join links.

The first implementation will centralize these read rules, show the actual schedule before proposing changes, and prevent a lesson-specific CTA from offering a different obligation. Financial mutations must re-resolve authorization and amounts from database records in the existing serialization boundary. Preview data has no authority over price, payer or coverage.

### Compatibility and policy decisions

- Keep [credit PRs #94–#97](https://github.com/coach-sudo/Studio-Portal/pull/97) separate. Their pending shared-pool reservations, strict service and duration typing, billing overrides and reminder receipts are useful interfaces. The projection should accept authoritative lesson-level coverage facts from those paths when they are approved. Preserve all accounting history and avoid duplicating their ledger or notification scheduler. [PR #93](https://github.com/coach-sudo/Studio-Portal/pull/93) remains independent; its calendar reconciliation can update occurrence state through canonical records.
- Full lesson balances are the unit of selection. Choosing two lessons leaves other invoice lines outstanding. The student cannot enter a custom amount. A remainder after verified historical coverage can be selected if it completely settles that lesson. A bundle without reliable line attribution needs review.
- Which unbilled lessons may create an obligation without the coach issuing an invoice? Main permits unclassified coach appointments and special pricing. Start with issued obligations; require an explicit service, price and payer rule before adding self-issuance. Never use today's catalog price for an existing lesson with unknown terms.
- When a new booking falls inside the lead-time window, or a reschedule brings the calculated date into the past, should the payer be offered immediate explicit payment or are other terms allowed? Default to manual review or a deliberate one-time payment offer. Do not attempt an immediate background charge on enrollment or a schedule change with that ambiguity.
- Define permitted lead-time range, charge time in the studio timezone, consent amount limits, retry and notice policy in studio configuration. Use test settings for implementation evidence. A changed service, increased amount beyond the mandate or changed designated payer needs a fresh review and consent.
- Preserve existing cancellation, refund, package expiration and fixed subscription terms. Where they do not define the post-payment outcome, surface review and retain the original receipt. Do not invent rollover, blanket refunds, or permission to book under another payer.
- Existing protected-hosted procedures require exact-head approval and synthetic identities. The Stripe connector currently requires reauthentication. Local mocks, disposable CI and prior payment evidence cannot establish acceptance for a new payment flow.
